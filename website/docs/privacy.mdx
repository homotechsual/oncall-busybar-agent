# Privacy policy

*Last updated: 2026-11-01*

**BUSY Bar on call agent** is a Windows tray app, published by homotechsual (MJCO), that runs on
your own machine. This page covers what data it reads, where that data goes and what it stores.
For what the app does, see [Getting started](/getting-started).

## Who we are

This app is published by **homotechsual** (MJCO). It comes with a shared, multi-tenant Entra app
registration so most people don't need to register their own. That registration doesn't give us
any access to your data. Microsoft issues sign-in tokens straight to the app on your device, and
they never pass through anything we run.

## What data is accessed

- **Your Teams presence** from Microsoft Graph (`Presence.Read`, delegated). The app checks it
  every 30 seconds by default (`Graph:PollIntervalSeconds`) to tell when you're on a call.
- **Your calendar** from Microsoft Graph (`Calendars.ReadBasic`, delegated). The app checks it at
  the same time, whether or not you're on a call, to set the timer to your current meeting's end.
  It asks Graph for each event's end time and nothing else, so subjects, attendees, locations and
  bodies never reach your machine. `Calendars.ReadBasic` can't read event bodies or attachments
  anyway.
- **Microphone activity on your machine.** This is Windows' own record of which apps have
  recently used the microphone. The app never hears or records audio. It uses this to catch calls
  Teams presence misses, such as a call in another app. You can turn it off with `Mic:Enabled`;
  see [Configuration](/configuration).

Both Graph permissions are delegated. They work under your own sign-in, with only the access your
organization already gives you.

## Where it goes

The app only talks to two things:

- **Microsoft Graph**, through Microsoft's own sign-in library (MSAL). Your organization's
  agreement with Microsoft covers that data, as it does for Teams itself.
- **Your BUSY Bar**, over USB or your local network. The app sends it a timer value and one of
  the device's built-in status themes. No meeting details or presence information reach the
  device.

There's no telemetry, analytics or crash reporting. Nothing about your presence, calendar or
microphone use goes anywhere else.

## What's stored locally

- **The sign-in token cache**, at `%LOCALAPPDATA%\oncall-busybar-agent\msal_cache.bin`, so you
  stay signed in between runs. Microsoft's sign-in library manages it, and Windows encrypts it for
  your user account.
- **Your settings file**, `appsettings.json`, in the same folder.

Both belong to your Windows user account and the app never sends them anywhere. The Store and
MSIX versions keep them in their package data
(`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\MJCOLtd.OnCallBusyBarAgent_<id>\LocalCache\Local\oncall-busybar-agent\`)
instead, and Windows deletes them when you uninstall it.

## Your choices

- **Sign out** from the tray icon's menu at any time. The app stops reading from Graph straight
  away.
- **Delete the token cache** (`msal_cache.bin`, above) to remove your sign-in from the device.
- **Revoke the app's access** at [My Apps](https://myapplications.microsoft.com/). This works even
  if you no longer have the device.
- **Turn off the microphone check** by setting `Mic:Enabled` to `false`. See
  [Configuration](/configuration).

## IT-managed deployments

If your organization deploys the app with Intune or Group Policy, IT can set its settings
centrally (see [Intune / Group Policy](/intune-group-policy)). They can set values, but they can't
read anything back through the app, and it doesn't change what data the app reads or where it
goes.
